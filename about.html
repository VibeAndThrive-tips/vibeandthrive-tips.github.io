About VibeAndThrive

Empowering better living through practical, evidence-based advice

Our Story

VibeAndThrive was founded with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to practical, trustworthy advice for living a healthier, happier, and more fulfilling life. In a world overloaded with conflicting wellness information, we set out to create a single destination where busy people can find actionable tips that actually work.

Since our launch, we have helped thousands of readers make meaningful improvements in their daily routines—from building better morning habits and managing stress to eating smarter and boosting productivity. Every piece of content on our site is carefully researched, reviewed for accuracy, and written in plain language so anyone can benefit.

Meet the Author

VibeAndThrive is written and maintained by Hari Kishan, a lifestyle and wellness writer who has spent several years studying evidence-based strategies for habit formation, productivity, nutrition, and mental well-being. Hari's approach is grounded in reading peer-reviewed research — from sources like the NIH, Harvard Health, Mayo Clinic, and PubMed — and translating that research into practical, jargon-free guidance for everyday people.

VibeAndThrive does not claim to provide professional medical, psychological, or financial advice. Every article is written from the perspective of a well-researched lifestyle guide, and readers are always encouraged to consult qualified professionals for personal health decisions. See our full disclaimer.

Important Health Disclaimer

The content on VibeAndThrive is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not intended as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read on this website.

Our Editorial Standards

Quality matters. Every article published on VibeAndThrive follows strict editorial guidelines:

1

Research-First Approach

All advice is grounded in peer-reviewed studies, expert consensus, and trusted health organisations. We reference sources including PubMed / NCBI, Mayo Clinic, Harvard Health Publishing, NIH, American Psychological Association (APA), Sleep Foundation, and WHO. Where we reference specific findings, articles include links directly to those sources.

2

Actionable & Practical

We focus on tips you can implement today — no vague motivational fluff. Every article includes step-by-step guidance and real-world examples that fit into busy everyday lives.

3

Transparent & Honest

We are not medical professionals. Our content is clearly framed as lifestyle guidance, not clinical advice. Every article carries a disclaimer, and we link to professional resources so readers can explore topics further with qualified guidance.

4

Regularly Updated

We review and refresh our articles to ensure accuracy and relevance. Outdated information is promptly corrected. Each article displays its last-reviewed date so readers always know how current the content is.

Trusted Sources We Use

Our wellness and personal growth content draws from the following peer-reviewed and institutional sources:

  • PubMed / NCBI — National Library of Medicine peer-reviewed research database
  • Mayo Clinic — Evidence-based health and wellness information
  • Harvard Health Publishing — Harvard Medical School's consumer health education
  • National Institutes of Health (NIH) — U.S. federal medical research authority
  • American Psychological Association (APA) — Psychological research and guidance
  • Sleep Foundation — Medically reviewed sleep research
  • World Health Organization (WHO) — Global public health guidance
