About the Author

Hari Kishan writes and edits practical guides about wellness, productivity, food, home living, travel, relationships, and personal growth. His work focuses on clear explanations, realistic routines, and small changes that readers can apply in everyday life.

Articles are researched using reputable public sources, including government health agencies, medical institutions, professional organizations, and peer-reviewed research databases. Health and nutrition content is educational and does not replace advice from a qualified professional.

Editorial Approach

  • Explain practical steps in plain language.
  • Link important health and safety claims to reputable sources.
  • Separate general education from individualized professional advice.
  • Review articles for clarity, accuracy, and continued relevance.
