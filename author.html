About Me

I am Hari Kishan, a software engineer based in Bangalore, India. I spend my working hours writing code and debugging systems — and my off hours thinking about food, routines, and how to live a little better without overcomplicating it.

I started cooking properly in college — partly out of necessity, partly because I genuinely enjoyed it. Over the years I have tried hundreds of recipes, adapted Indian home-cooking techniques for small kitchens and tight budgets, and written food reviews for restaurants and home-cooked meals alike. That background is the foundation for every cooking and food article on this site.

Working in software also gave me a front-row seat to the challenges of maintaining focus, managing stress, and actually switching off at the end of the day. I went through the typical cycle — trying every productivity framework, chasing optimization — before settling on simpler, more sustainable approaches. Most of the wellness and productivity writing here comes from that lived experience of figuring out what actually works in a full-time, deadline-driven job.

I started VibeAndThrive because most lifestyle advice I found online was either too abstract or written for a very different context than urban Indian life. The goal here is practical guides that respect your time, your budget, and your actual circumstances.

How I Approach Every Article

  • Write from experience first. If I have not tried something myself, I say so clearly. Personal context is always disclosed.
  • Keep claims honest. Health and nutrition content avoids exaggerated promises. Advice is framed as guidance, not guarantees.
  • Make it actionable. Every article ends with something the reader can actually do today, not just think about.
  • Respect the Indian context. Prices, ingredients, and lifestyle references reflect what is actually available and affordable here, not imported advice repackaged.
  • No medical advice. All health-related content is general lifestyle information and always recommends a qualified professional for individual concerns.
