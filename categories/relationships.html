Keys to Healthy Relationships

Communication

Open, honest, and respectful communication is the bedrock of every healthy relationship.

  • Active listening without judgment
  • Express needs clearly
  • Regular check-ins with loved ones

Trust

Trust is built through consistency, reliability, and follow-through on commitments.

  • Keep promises
  • Be transparent and honest
  • Show up when it matters

Empathy

Understanding others' perspectives strengthens connections and resolves conflicts peacefully.

  • Validate others' feelings
  • See situations from their viewpoint
  • Respond with compassion

Boundaries

Healthy boundaries protect your energy and create space for authentic connection.

  • Know your limits
  • Communicate them clearly
  • Respect others' boundaries too
