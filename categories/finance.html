Pillars of Financial Wellness

Earn

Maximize your income through skill development, side projects, and career growth strategies.

  • Negotiate fair compensation
  • Develop high-demand skills
  • Explore additional income streams

Save

Build an emergency fund and save consistently toward short-term and long-term goals.

  • Automate your savings
  • 3-6 month emergency fund
  • Goal-based saving accounts

Budget

Track your spending, allocate your income wisely, and make sure every dollar has a purpose.

  • Track all expenses
  • 50/30/20 or zero-based method
  • Monthly financial review

Protect

Safeguard your financial future with insurance, debt management, and smart planning.

  • Adequate insurance coverage
  • Manage and reduce debt
  • Plan for the unexpected
